## PART 1 — The 8 abstractions

### 1. Latent prediction (the world-model war)
**Essence:** move the training target from token space to latent space —
predict *representations of what happens next*, not the pixels/tokens
themselves (JEPA family, Genie 3, "NextLat" next-latent prediction for
coherent transformer belief states).
**Who:** LeCun-lineage JEPAs (I-/V-/Causal-), Google DeepMind (Genie 3),
LeWorldModel, "JEPA-Anything"; framing everywhere as the "2026 architecture
war" (turingpost, sterlites, builtin roundups).
**Why deep:** replaces next-token as THE objective with next-*state*; the
field's umbrella term for every world-model effort.
**Fleet synergy:** this is the elephant's home turf (personal JEPA over the
room-field, VibeTrajectory d_mu gestures, jev_field_watch regime alarms) and
quilt-jepa's (E7 V-JEPA KEEP through the cell contract). G1 is literally
next-latent prediction on a discrete glyph lattice.

### 2. Depth as a loop (recurrent-depth / looped transformers)
**Essence:** one shared block applied R times — inference-time depth without
parameters, with adaptive halting deciding how long each input "thinks."
**Who:** OpenAI Astra (recurrent-depth reports; Raschka analysis
sebastianraschka.com/blog/2026/openai-astra-looped-transformers.html),
LoopFormer (elastic-depth, ICLR 2026, loopformer.github.io), Nanbeige 4.2
(22-layer stack ×2 open-weight), LoopSpec (pipelined speculative decoding for
looped transformers, Sep 16).
**Why deep:** unparameters compute — the parameter/compute tradeoff becomes a
runtime dial; frontier labs are shipping it.
**Fleet synergy:** the D2 free-delta skeleton is exactly the kind of map you
iterate; the nursery's 12L model makes the R=2 experiment a one-night paired
run. If free-delta composes with looping, both abstractions are "compute from
reuse."

### 3. Multiplication-free ternary arithmetic
**Essence:** {-1,0,+1} weights trained-in from scratch (BitNet) — inference is
adds/subtracts only; matured in 2026 with official GPU kernels, CPU opts
(bitnet.cpp), **BitNet-embedding models (Jul 2026)**, and CAT-Q post-training
ternarization (~100,000× fewer training tokens than QAT).
**Who:** Microsoft Research (bitnet repo, b1.58 2B4T), CAT-Q authors (ICML
2026 poster), "intelligence density" framing across edge-ML press.
**Why deep:** kills the multiplier — the last sacred op — and makes CPU/edge
native inference a first-class citizen.
**Fleet synergy:** the fleet already speaks ternary (JEV ternary embeddings,
D19/D20 ternary transitions) and free-delta's win was removing multiplicative
mixing from attention. The two stories complete each other: delta IS adds,
ternary IS adds.

### 4. Verifiable environments as the RL bottleneck
**Essence:** agentic progress is now gated by environment/verifier quality,
not compute — open hubs (PrimeIntellect Environments Hub: 2,500+ envs +
verifiers library; HF×Meta OpenEnv) and the "environment bubble" (GPUs idle
waiting on env rollouts; amazon.science paper).
**Who:** PrimeIntellect, Hugging Face + Meta (huggingface.co/blog/openenv),
ReVeal (self-verification for code agents), rl-list.com cataloging.
**Why deep:** relocates the scarcity — the unit of progress becomes "a
submittable task with a machine-checkable success criterion," which is also
exactly what a falsifier is.
**Fleet synergy:** the falsifier campaign method is verifier-first by
construction; receipts are machine-checkable; quilt-arcade's judge and the
pincher→lever-runner lane (no-LLM-in-shell, Critic as check) are a
deterministic environment harness already running.

### 5. Autonomous research loops — and their mode collapse
**Essence:** agents author-and-rank training mutations end-to-end
(auto-nanogpt, nanochat's 100+ agent patches, METR's AI-RD measurement) — and
a new literature is emerging on the failure mode: algorithmic mode collapse in
code-level research loops ("Beneath the Diff," EMNLP 2026).
**Who:** Karpathy (nanochat), PrimeIntellect (auto-nanogpt), OpenAI
(research-acceleration post), METR; EMNLP 2026 for the collapse diagnosis.
**Why deep:** the research process itself becomes a trainable/observable
system with its own dynamics (diversity collapse, reward hacking) — a
meta-level of the speedrun.
**Fleet synergy:** the nursery IS this loop at 4050 scale — D2's KEEP is one
accepted agent-authored mutation. The falsifier campaign (pre-registered
kills, FAIL-first, both-numbers) is a designed anti-collapse device nobody in
the field is running.

### 6. Canvas generation (diffusion LMs go commercial)
**Essence:** generate a whole block at once and iteratively refine it
(bidirectional context, parallel decode) instead of strict left-to-right —
Mercury 2.5 (Sep 2026, +40% intelligence over Mercury 2), DiffusionGemma
(Jun 2026, 256-token canvas, ~4× faster), NVIDIA two-tower (2.4× at 98.7%
quality).
**Who:** Inception Labs (Mercury line), Google (DiffusionGemma), NVIDIA;
kuleshov-group build guides; sander.ai continuous-DLM writeup (Aug 2026).
**Why deep:** replaces sequential generation as the default; makes
"persistence + refinement" a legitimate architecture shape.
**Fleet synergy:** G1's INCONCLUSIVE was "persistence wins on slow-motion
sources" — the canvas paradigm starts *from* persistence and refines. The
chiaroscuro V=32 lattice is literally a small canvas; denoising 48×36 glyph
frames is cheap on a 4050.

### 7. Representation steering (model state as dials)
**Essence:** activation directions as causal control knobs — find a direction
in representation space, add/remove it, and steer behavior with minimal
side-effects ("Minimally Invasive Steering," Sep 25; LocUS head-selection +
subspace steering, Sep 28).
**Who:** steering/SAE-adjacent groups (Minimally Invasive Steering, LocUS,
CG-Probes); interpretability-to-control pipeline broadly.
**Why deep:** turns interpretability's read-only probes into write access —
model internals become an interface, not just an X-ray.
**Fleet synergy:** the elephant's whole idiom is dials (d_mu, twist/planarity
gesture axes, jev_field_watch regime alarms). Steering makes the metaphor
measurable: do axis-aligned directions actually move the room-field readouts?

### 8. Weights as data (weight-space grammars)
**Essence:** treat weight matrices and their *deltas* as first-class
learnable objects — "Weight Pair Encoding: Inducing a Smaller Grammar in
Neural Network Weights" (arXiv, Sep 28), BitNet-embedding of weights, model
editing, hypernetwork momentum.
**Who:** weight-grammar authors (fresh arXiv), Microsoft (BitNet-embeddings),
model-editing community.
**Why deep:** the model stops being the dataset's output and becomes the
dataset — structure in weight space is learnable, transferable, and
predictive of behavior.
**Fleet synergy:** the nursery's receipt ledger (every mutation diff +
keep/kill verdict, seeds pinned) is a labeled weight-delta corpus nobody else
has; D2 is itself a claim that *differences of the computation* carry the
signal.

**Runner-up buzz (fold into the above):** agent memory that learns
(vectorize-io/hindsight trending; ai-memory CLIs) — relevant to fleet-memory
and elephant's "every reading is someone's reading," but the eight above are
where the deep water is this fortnight.

## PART 2 — The gem-list (ranked)

Each line: **abstraction × fleet asset → falsifiable question · 4050 cost ·
gem potential.** Pre-register the gate before any run (house rule).

1. **Diff-JEPA** (latent-prediction × D2 free-delta + quilt-jepa): predictor
   targets the latent *difference* d_z = z_{t+1} − z_t instead of z_{t+1} —
   does diff-target beat state-target on held-out transition gap at matched
   budget? · ~2–3 h · **GEM: unifies the fleet's two independent wins into one
   claim — "predict differences, not states" — testable at sequence scale
   (D2) and latent scale (JEPA). If both hold, that's a family, not a trick.**
2. **Looped free-delta** (depth-as-loop × D2): one shared free-delta block
   looped R=2 at equal params vs the 12-block baseline — val_bpb win or no?
   · ~3 h paired · **GEM: Astra-adjacent architecture family with receipts
   nobody else can produce; if reuse-of-parameters and reuse-of-compute
   compose with the delta operator, three abstractions collapse into one
   skeleton.**
3. **Ternary free-delta** (multiplication-free × D2 + JEV): BitLinear-ize the
   free-delta skeleton — does the −0.029 bpb paired win survive {-1,0,+1}
   weights? · ~6 h overnight · **GEM: a fully multiplier-free LM (additive
   attention + ternary weights) trained on one 6 GB GPU; within ~5% of FP16
   baseline it's a headline and the boat-doctrine endpoint.**
4. **Persistence-seeded glyph diffusion** (canvas × G1/chiaroscuro): tiny
   discrete refiner starts from the persistence canvas, denoises toward the
   next V=32 frame, judged by the SAME pre-registered G1 gate — does
   refinement beat AR exactly on the slow-motion sources where G1 lost?
   · ~3–4 h · **GEM: converts G1's INCONCLUSIVE into a paradigm comparison
   (AR vs refinement) on the field's only generative glyph-video corpus.**
5. **Δ-magnitude adaptive halting** (depth-as-loop × D2): per-token
   accumulated delta-norm as the loop's halting signal — more compute where
   the residual is still moving; beats fixed-depth val_bpb-per-FLOP or no?
   · ~3 h · GEM: wires "difference" to "thinking time" — a measured bridge
   between the two hottest abstractions, cheap to kill.
6. **Verifier-packaged nursery** (verifiable-envs × nursery): wrap each
   mutation as an OpenEnv-style env with a machine-checkable paired-seed
   verifier — does keep-yield per GPU-hour rise over one week vs the ad-hoc
   loop? · ~2 h GPU + CPU · GEM: the falsifier method becomes a drop-in node
   in the field's verification stack — citable infrastructure, not just
   internal hygiene.
7. **Collapse telemetry, pre-registered kill** (research-loops × falsifier
   campaign): track proposal-entropy + accepted-mutation diversity across
   nursery generations; if entropy sits below floor N generations the loop is
   declared collapsed — does the field's mode-collapse reproduce at 4050

   reproduction test of research-loop collapse; feeds the EMNLP 2026

8. **Steering the elephant** (steering × elephant/lucid): extract steering
   directions on Qwen3-4B, align them to VibeTrajectory axes (d_mu / twist /
   planarity) — do axis-aligned directions predict jev_field_watch regime

   dial metaphor into a measured control mechanism for room-perception —
   "dials that actually dial."
9. **Belief-state probe on delta residuals** (latent-prediction × D2): linear
   NextLat-style probe reading next-latent structure from free-delta vs
   baseline residuals — does the delta operator's residual carry more
   transition structure? · ~2 h · GEM: the mechanism section for WHY
   free-delta wins — differences as world-model state.
10. **Weight-grammar mining of the mutation ledger** (weights-as-data ×
    receipts): keep/kill predicted at AUC > 0.7 from receipt diff-grammar
    features alone? · ~1 h · GEM: the fleet's experiment history becomes a

11. **Ternary reflex distillation for pincher** (multiplication-free ×
    pincher/lever-runner): distill (state→command) loop history into a
    ternary reflex net firing <50 ms no-LLM — hit-rate ≥ LLM-authored .nail
    rules on replay? · ~2 h · GEM: the cellular loop loses its last LLM

12. **Ternary glyph embeddings** (multiplication-free × chiaroscuro): 1.58-bit
    embedding of glyph frames — does the c1 bar (scene-param readout

## PART 3 — Top 3 gems

1. **Diff-JEPA (#1)** — the deepest unification available to us: if the
   difference operator that won D2/D3 also wins as a JEPA target, "predict
   differences, not states" stops being a speedrun mutation and becomes a
   principle with two independent confirmations. Cost: one evening.
2. **Looped free-delta (#2)** — rides the Astra/LoopFormer wave with the only
   honest small-scale paired harness in sight; the composition question
   (delta × weight-sharing) is open in the literature and answerable here in
   3 hours.
3. **Ternary free-delta (#3)** — the multiplier-free endgame: additive
   attention on ternary weights, trained overnight on the 4050. Either the
   delta signal survives (headline) or it doesn't (a real boundary on
   multiplication-free training, still a receipt).

Honorable mention: **persistence-seeded glyph diffusion (#4)** — it's the
correct next move for G1 regardless of gem value, and it's the only experiment
here that also produces a public dataset artifact.

**The one-sentence map:** the field's deep moves this fortnight are all
"replace a spent abstraction with a cheaper loop" — tokens→latents, depth→
loops, multipliers→adds, environments→verifiers, papers→mutation loops — and
every one of those loops runs for free overnight on the 4050, where the
fleet's difference operator is already sitting at the center of all of them.
