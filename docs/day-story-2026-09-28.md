# What Grew in the Cleared Ground
*the GPU lab, 2026-09-28*

The day began with a demolition.

The falsifier campaign — the one built to kill the week's favorite finding — finished its work by noon. X1 licensed the thesis (the dials explain 0.43 of the embedding, mean-weighted); then X3 killed its body (volume and presence are classical image statistics in disguise); X6 killed its reach (the read is range-local: train below 0.7, and the world above 0.7 is unreadable, mood at −3.15); X2 found it renderer-bound (cross-renderer transfer fails on every dial, volume anti-transfers below the shuffle null); and X9, the last, found the "generalization gap" was only a nonlinearity gap — a 20-dim statistics battery with an MLP reads presence at 0.895 against the encoder's 0.835. Five probes, four kills, one license. The staged room had measured the staging.

By the doctrine this is the product: failures as first-class content, every kill reproduction-verified. But it left the ground cleared — nothing standing where the morning's thesis had been.

What grew there instead, in order:

At 13:17, free-delta. A one-line change to the training loop — predict the difference between tokens, not the tokens — won by 0.0293 bits per byte at zero new parameters, a hundred times the margin of the delta-attention attempt before it. At seed 1337 it won again (−0.0286), two paired wins against a baseline of 1.6959, six times seed noise. The difference operator had its first trained-model evidence, and the replication made it a receipt instead of a rumor.

The glyph program ran the other direction: up from pixels. G1 trained the fleet's first glyph-domain model (25.3M parameters, hand-built end-to-end after four lane deaths — the phantoms of the rate-limit storms, reported wins with no artifacts, which taught the lab its hardest rule: no artifact, no relay). G4 masked the loss to changed cells and tripled the learning of dynamics (life 0.056→0.198). G5 composed the two experts — persistence for statics, the diff head for dynamics — behind a confidence threshold that is, itself, a dial: statics/dynamics, confirm/predict. G7 gave it time (600 seconds) and it cracked what capacity and data could not (life 0.422, mandelbrot 0.320). G10 ran G7 again at a different seed and landed within noise on every source. Time-cracks-hard-dynamics is double-seeded. Propose, replicate, harden.

The walls got their honesty too. G6 starved the data pipeline and nothing improved — the hard dynamics are not data-bound. G8 doubled the time and the curves plateaued. And the params axis died five times in a row, each death diagnosed: depth drags width (85.1M parameters, OOM), width pins heads (odd-head assert), the patch script itself died twice on quote-nesting and scope, and the fifth death was the wall itself — 85M does not fit six gigabytes at batch two. The skeleton's knobs are coupled; the axis is boxed; three deaths on one axis is itself a finding about the geometry. No obituary was written for a wall. They were all booked.

Then the insect showed up.

It had been there all along — the clearing had made room to see it. The chiaroscuro renderer is a compound eye in silico: the glyph lattice trades space for time, and the probe that measured the trade (c3) found it free — the tie between framerate and pixel budget costs nothing. The edge families {0°,45°,90°,135°} are the lobula plate's preferred-axis structure. The fly's own numbers arrived from the primary literature: 58 lobula-plate neurons per hemisphere (Zhao 2023, the Janelia EM survey) — and T4/T5's four cardinal classes are exactly the four motion codes the next experiment (ie1) had already specced. The Reichardt correlator's response was derived and verified on paper — quadratic in contrast, band-pass at ωτ=1, the sign-flip at the aliasing edge, the reverse-phi inversion — and the delay filter's time constant adapts with light level. τ is not a constant; it is a dial. The fifth dimension — the resolution-to-framerate axis — is biologically real, and the fly tunes it.

The learning side came with it, primary-sourced through a dead search quota (PubMed and Europe PMC answered when the search engine would not): ~150 projection neurons expand into ~2000 Kenyon cells by random sparse convergence, fifteen compartments each with its own output and dopamine pair, plasticity gated strictly on the temporal order of pre-synapse-before-dopamine — and the third factor is a broadcast, not a per-synapse gate. Six percent of the synapses get direct dopamine contact. The reward signal is a bus. The lab had been running one for months: receipts, broadcast to lanes, gating what persists.

The field's own critique of the insect-brain-plays-DOOM buzz (Kording et al.: without independently verifiable metrics, skepticism is difficult to rebut) answered itself in the mirror: receipts are the independently verifiable metrics. The falsifier and the insect converged on the same fix.

So the day ended with a keel.

The day's data says predict differences, not states — at the token level (free-delta, twice), at the target level (diff-mask, replicated), and the latent-level test is the next fire (K1: diff-target vs state-target over frozen V-JEPA 2 latents, gate frozen, the whole backcast falsifiable down to a clean KILL). The method got a name and a discipline: write the far-future receipt first — dated, verdict KEEP, numbers in it — then extract the gates backward from it, and build toward the receipt or die trying. And the chip began measuring its own soil: a probe and a router now sit between every experiment and the silicon — the GPU serializes (the sibling rule, learned the hard way, five times), the twenty-four threads flex around it, and nothing asks for a device anymore; everything asks for compute.

We are taking root. The ground was cleared by noon; by nightfall the roots had found the walls and grown down along them.
